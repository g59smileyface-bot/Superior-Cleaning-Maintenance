Maintenance Services

Whether it’s minor repairs, upkeep, or odd jobs, our team is here to take care of the things that often get pushed aside. Reliable, friendly, and efficient—we help you stay on top of the details so you can focus on what matters most.

Get a quote today!

Our Maintenance Packages:

Basic - "Essential Upkeep"

Perfect for routine maintenance and quick fixes:

  • Light handyman tasks (e.g., tightening fixtures, replacing light bulbs)

  • Lawn mowing and basic garden tidy-up

  • Rubbish removal (small loads)

  • Monthly visit or on-demand booking

Standard - "Reliable Routine"

Ideal for clients who want consistent support and a broader range of services:

  • Everything in the Basic Package

  • Minor repairs (e.g., patching walls, fixing doors)

  • Pressure washing (driveways, patios)

  • Window cleaning (ground level)

  • Bi-weekly or monthly scheduling

Premium - "Complete Care"

Designed for busy households or small businesses needing full-service attention:

  • Everything in the Standard Package

  • Interior and exterior maintenance checks

  • Seasonal garden care and landscaping touch-ups

  • Gutter cleaning and roof inspection

  • Priority booking and flexible scheduling
