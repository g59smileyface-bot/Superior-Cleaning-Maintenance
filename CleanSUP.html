Cleaning Services

At Superior, we specialise in both residential and commercial cleaning services—tailored to suit every need. Whether it’s a routine tidy-up or a deep clean, we’re committed to leaving your space spotless, no matter the situation.

Get a quote today!

Our Cleaning Packages:

Basic - "Fresh Start"

Perfect for light, routine cleaning to keep spaces tidy and refreshed:

  • Surface dusting and vacuuming

  • Kitchen wipe-down (benchtops, sinks, appliances)

  • Bathroom refresh (toilets, mirrors, sinks)

  • Floor cleaning (sweep and mop)

  • Weekly or fortnightly scheduling

Standard - "Thorough Touch"

Ideal for homes or businesses needing deeper, more consistent care:

  • Everything in the Basic Package

  • Deep bathroom and kitchen cleaning (grout, fixtures, appliances)

  • Window cleaning (interior)

  • Cobweb removal and skirting board wipe-down

  • Flexible scheduling: weekly, fortnightly, or monthly

Premium - "Spotless Guarantee"

Designed for high-traffic spaces or clients who want full-service attention:

  • Everything in the Standard Package

  • Carpet steam cleaning or upholstery refresh

  • Exterior window cleaning

  • Wall spot cleaning and high-touch surface sanitisation

  • Priority booking and custom scheduling options
